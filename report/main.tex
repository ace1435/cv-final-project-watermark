\section{Method}

\paragraph{Data.}
1,000 images, 500 photographs from CLIC 2020~\citep{clic2020} and 500 screen images, application screenshots from ScreenSpot-v2~\citep{wu2024osatlas} and ScreenSpot-Pro~\citep{li2025screenspotpro}, web pages from VisualWebBench~\citep{liu2024visualwebbench} and document pages from DocLayNet~\citep{pfitzmann2022doclaynet} and arXiv, all resized to 1024 px on the long side. A disjoint null set of 5,000 unwatermarked images, including DIV2K~\citep{agustsson2017div2k}, sets the thresholds. \todo{One sentence on the 50 image smoke subset if space allows.}

\paragraph{Sharing actions.}
75 conditions in 16 families. 45 single actions, JPEG, downscale, upscale, crop, shift, letterbox, caption, sticker, brightness, contrast, gamma and rotation at realistic levels plus one harsh level. Five platform profiles that reduce to the JPEG quality of Telegram, X, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook, and the same five on a simulated 2048 px original. 15 real screenshots rendered by headless Chromium through Playwright~\citep{playwright} at browser zooms from 90 to 150 percent, Windows display scaling, a Mac retina screen and two phones, a partial screenshot and the browser JPEG. Five chains of these. Every action records its exact parameters and an inverse into the original pixel grid. \todo{Trim to fit.}

\paragraph{Detection protocol.}
The score is the matched filter between the decoder logits and the embedded message. The threshold per method is the value that 1 percent of the 5,000 null images exceed, so every rate is a TPR at 1 percent FPR with a Wilson interval~\citep{wilson1927}. Every image is decoded three times. Rung 1 as received. Rung 2 after the oracle undoes the recorded geometry and fills lost regions with the unwatermarked cover. Rung 3 after the geometry is estimated from the received image and the original with DISK keypoints~\citep{tyszkiewicz2020disk}, LightGlue~\citep{lindenberger2023lightglue} in Kornia~\citep{riba2020kornia} and an ECC refinement~\citep{evangelidis2008ecc}. A failure at rung 1 that rung 2 repairs is an alignment failure, a failure that even the oracle cannot repair is destruction. Rung 3 uses its own threshold from realigned null images, and families where a sharing action pushed more than 2 percent of null images over the threshold also carry a per family threshold.
